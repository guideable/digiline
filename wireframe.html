SECTION 8: TECHNICAL ADVANTAGES (White Background) - Expandable Accordions

8 Clinical & Engineering Advantages of DAD-DAM

High-level benefits with expandable technical depth for those who want it

1. Superior Anatomical Adaptation [+]

Benefit: Better fit and improved orthodontic tooth movement (OTM)

[Expandable: Fewer attachments required, better expression of OTM due to precise geometry. Direct-print maintains designed shape without thermoforming distortion, resulting in superior tooth contact and force transfer.]

2. Advanced Design Capabilities [+]

Benefit: Enhanced biomechanics with precise control of thickness and forces

[Expandable: Variable-thickness design protocols enable precise force management. CT scan validation shows ±90% accuracy vs. 40-60% random loss in thermoforming. 1x1mm geometric modification = 60% force increase (50→80g), demonstrating exact biomechanical control.]

3. Superior Material with Shape Memory [+]

Benefit: Biocompatible material with ideal force delivery and heat-activated shape memory

[Expandable: FDA-cleared biocompatible resin (<0.1 ppm residual monomer). Delivers low, constant ideal force for continuous tooth movement. Heat-activated shape memory allows reactivation via freeze-drying technology, maintaining force longer than thermoformed aligners.]

4. Enhanced Patient Experience [+]

Benefit: Better comfort, easier insertion and removal, biological considerations

[Expandable: Easy insertion whether heated or not. Easy removal reduces patient frustration. Biocompatible material considers biological safety. Superior anatomical adaptation = improved comfort and compliance throughout treatment.]

5. Manufacturing Advantages [+]

Benefit: More automation, less labor dependency, shorter turnaround

[Expandable: One person operates 4 printers producing 150 aligners/hour. Highly automated workflow reduces labor costs and human error. In-practice manufacturing = days not weeks turnaround, eliminating shipping delays.]

6. Environmental Impact [+]

Benefit: Dramatically reduced plastic and resin waste

[Expandable: 60g+ plastic waste saved per case (aligner waste reduction). Eliminates thermoforming model waste (resin waste reduction). No truckload shipping = reduced carbon footprint. 75% overall plastic reduction vs. centralized thermoforming.]

7. Clinical Workflow Benefits [+]

Benefit: Less chair time, fewer refinements, more predictable outcomes

[Expandable: Less chair time placing/removing attachments (fewer needed). Less chair time with refinements (50% fewer required). Shorter treatment time due to more predictable outcomes from precise force control. Grin® remote monitoring reduces in-person visits by 2/3.]

8. Economic Advantages [+]

Benefit: Lower costs through fewer refinements and reduced chair time

[Expandable: Fewer refinements = reduced material and labor costs. Less chair time = higher practice productivity and profitability. Product margins stay in-house vs. surrendered to centralized labs. Compete with offshore pricing at superior quality.]
